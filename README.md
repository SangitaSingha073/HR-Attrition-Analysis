# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

Analyzing why employees leave the company using the IBM HR Analytics dataset.

---

## Objective

To find the key reasons behind employee attrition and suggest ways to improve retention.

---

## Dataset

- **Source:** IBM HR Analytics Dataset (Kaggle)
- **Rows:** 1,470 employees
- **Columns:** 35 features (Age, Salary, Department, Overtime, etc.)

---

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## Steps Followed

1. Loaded and explored the dataset
2. Cleaned the data and handled data types
3. Analyzed key factors affecting attrition
4. Created 12 visualizations
5. Wrote business insights and recommendations

---

## Key Findings

- Overall attrition rate is **16.1%**
- Employees doing **overtime** are 3x more likely to leave
- **Sales department** has the highest attrition (20.6%)
- Employees who left earned **30% less** than those who stayed
- **Young employees (18–25)** leave the most (34.2%)

---

## Recommendations

- Reduce overtime workload
- Revise salary for junior roles
- Improve onboarding for new joiners
- Create career growth programs for young employees
